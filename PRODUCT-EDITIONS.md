# CariFive — one-time purchase, optional upgrades

## Commercial model
- **Buy once:** The customer retains the purchased installed version indefinitely. No subscription, activation key, periodic validation or forced renewal is needed to keep that version running.
- **Optional new-feature upgrades:** $48 per 12 months of optional upgrade access (not a mandatory subscription), or purchase an individual major upgrade at its separately disclosed release price. At term end, the currently installed version continues running.
- **Support:** Installation help, custom work and problems demonstrably caused by unsupported local code changes may be charged separately, subject to applicable law and an agreed quotation.
- **Included obligations:** Statutory defect remedies, conformity and required security updates must not be rebranded as payable optional feature upgrades.
- **AI accounts:** Customers may need to sign into Codex, Claude or other selected providers or configure their API credentials. Provider bills are separate from the CariFive software price.
- **Purchase evidence:** Retain order/transaction receipts in a seller-controlled system. No local client file, checkbox or source code edit can prove a purchase or authorize an upgrade download. The local runtime must never require an online receipt check to remain operational.
- **Version delivery:** Seller-controlled downloads determine which purchased version/upgrade is delivered. No silent automatic installation of a paid upgrade, forced rollback or remote kill switch. Signed distribution, checksums and rollback remain desirable for safe updates.
- **Pricing still undecided:** The initial software purchase price and the price of a single major upgrade are not specified. Do not invent numbers in UI or checkout.
- **No implemented commerce claim:** This repository exposes the installed-version policy and local product behavior; live payment, invoice/receipt issue, entitlement-to-download and update distribution require separately verified commerce integration.

## Standard — five workspaces
- Five stable, local, owner-reviewed workspaces, distinct from Portfolio projects.
- Users can initialize Standard project slots without entering a software license key.
- The Control Centre 3 interface uses the same source review, CariFive execution, permissions and audit infrastructure as Portfolio.
- Pack guidance remains pending unless actually assigned and reviewed; the absence of a paid license key is not evidence of specific commercial pack rights.
- Interface: `/v3/?edition=standard`, with a legacy `/standard/` page linking to that functional interface.

## Portfolio — independently scoped workspaces
- Existing portfolio projects remain intact, with their specific knowledge mappings and files unchanged.
- No mandatory recurring payment or activation needed to retain installed-version access.
- New workspaces are additive and governed by the existing project capacity limit.
- Interface: `/v3/?edition=custom` or the legacy `/custom/` page.

## Non-negotiable safeguards
- Do not send, publish, spend, book, sign contracts or deploy without scoped authorization and a verified external receipt.
- Local editability is not a waiver of consumer rights or an automatic transfer of liability to the customer.
- Do not treat unlocked client code as proof of a purchase. The business should sell trustworthy distributions and optional upgrades, not obstruct local use.
- Real AI provider login and functional-output QA remain independent of this billing model.
