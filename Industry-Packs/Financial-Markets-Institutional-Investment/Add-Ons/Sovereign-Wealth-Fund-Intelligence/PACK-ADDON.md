# Sovereign Wealth Fund Intelligence — Specialized Add-on

**Industry:** Financial Markets & Institutional Investment Intelligence  
**Requires Core Pack:** `Industry-Packs/Financial-Markets-Institutional-Investment/PACK-OVERVIEW.md`  
**License scope:** Research methods and workflows. AIBO add-on ownership does **not** grant access to GSI's proprietary dataset, paid exports or research subscriptions.

## Components
1. `Verified Transactions`: investor-specific amount (with currency/unit/basis), headline transaction/round amount separately, announcement versus closing, deployment evidence, event/claim IDs, original source family.
2. `Investing Entities & Vehicles`: fund/parent, subsidiaries, bidcos, co-investors, investment vehicle domicile, beneficial ownership chain, confidence and effective dates. Review examples (Luxinva, MC Pomona Bidco, Masdar structures) rather than hardcode as independently verified facts.
3. `Regulatory & Structural Implications`: FDI and EU FSR, tender-offer filings, competition approvals, entity/asset domicile and geographic exposure; each claim requires jurisdiction, date and source.
4. `Mandate Behaviour`: investor sector, stage, ticket behavior only when supported by multiple transaction-specific records; do not assume willingness to fundraise proposals.
5. `Coverage & Research Backlog`: month × investor/source-group coverage, partial review, missing vehicle mapping and settlement verification.

## Audience and access
- Public/press: limited claim-verification briefs; attribution requested, not a substitute for license terms.
- Commercial: company research tools and decision-ready, source-scoped allocator profiles behind proper GSI entitlement.
- Academic: versioned historical extracts and methodology under institutional data rights.
- Investment teams: deeper dossiers and ongoing change monitoring.

## Mandatory safeguards
Public claims may cite source documents but never disclose proprietary row-level GSI research data by default. A press release of a round does not establish the SWF's ticket. An observed historical investment does not prove current capital allocation, current mandate or deal availability. Claim-level verification is required before decision-grade certification.
