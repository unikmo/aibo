# Connections, permission boundaries and risk controls

All actual accounts and authorisations live in `System/CONNECTIONS.md`. This file lists useful integration categories; **no provider is implied connected**.

## Priority integrations

1. CRM: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive — opportunity stages, account notes, deduplication and relationship history.
2. Proposals/docs: PandaDoc, Qwilr, Google Docs, Notion — approved SOW templates, version history, signatures (approval-gated).
3. Delivery: Asana, ClickUp, Monday, Notion — milestones, ownership, workload and evidence of completion.
4. Calendar/video: discovery, reviews, delivery meetings — read availability where authorised; invitations/changes affecting others are approval-gated.
5. Accounting: Xero, QuickBooks, FreshBooks — read reporting where granted; invoices/payment commitments are approval-gated.
6. Email/LinkedIn: approved outreach account, thread tracking and suppression; use channel-specific rules; research-based outreach delegation does not bypass platform or national law.

## Approval table

| Activity | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Research, qualification, proposal draft, internal KPI report, nonconsequential QA | Allowed within project and data permissions |
| **Research-led prospect outreach** | Delegated only after every condition in `AGENTS.md` and `System/CONNECTIONS.md` is met; otherwise draft only |
| Ordinary client emails, appointments/invitations, social posts, website publishing | Owner / authorised approver required |
| Pricing, discounts, unusual concessions, nonstandard SOW / contractual commitments | Owner / authorised approver required |
| Scope changes, statements implying a binding deliverable or new fee | Owner / authorised approver required |
| Public outcomes, cases, client logos, testimonials, case-study publishing | Evidence and client rights checked; owner approval required |
| Subcontractor/associate hiring or spend | Owner approval required |
| Client-confidential information, access sharing, retention changes | Follow legal/contractual policy; escalate when unclear |
| Invoice, payment request, booking, production deployment | Owner / authorised approver required |

## Escalation flags

Unclear legal permission for unsolicited commercial contact; confidentiality or intellectual property; employment/worker classification; sector-specific professional advice; sensitive data; adverse conflicts of interest; guarantees, regulated professional claims or performance claims; unauthorised access; financial or legal commitments; scope erosion; unverified testimonial.
