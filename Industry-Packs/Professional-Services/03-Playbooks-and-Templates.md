# Reusable workflows

All templates are in `Templates/`; use verified project facts only and keep approval gates visible.

1. **ICP + messaging matrix:** capture segments, decision makers, urgency, triggers, value case, proof and no-fit criteria. Research recipients before outreach and check contact eligibility.
2. **Discovery agenda + fit scorecard:** objective, stakeholders, current state, stakes, budget/timing, decision process and mutual next step. Identify non-fit early.
3. **Proposal/SOW:** business issue → measurable objectives → activities/deliverables → client dependencies → acceptance → timeline → fee model/expenses → exclusions → variation policy → risk flags → signature approval. Model assumptions and delivery effort; avoid invented price points.
4. **Onboarding:** contract and DPA/access checks, introduction, kickoff, shared plan, reporting cadence, baseline and data minimisation.
5. **Project kickoff + weekly status:** outcomes, milestones, decisions, RAG risks, change requests, budget/burn and next owner action.
6. **Retainer review/QBR:** value achieved, KPIs vs baseline, utilisation and overages, roadmap, renewal and expansion options; no promises without approval.
7. **Case study/testimonial capture:** written client permission, source numbers, attribution, confidentiality redaction and publication gate.
8. **Knowledge reuse:** classify deliverables by problem, sector, methodology and consent class; separate approved reusable IP from confidential client-specific material.
9. **Research-led outreach:** document ICP, company-specific trigger, relevant service hypothesis and lawful contact eligibility, then send only through verified project sender within the standing delegation in `AGENTS.md`. Log outcome and suppress objections.

**Review loop:** Research → Draft → Specialist critical review → Soni checks scope/evidence/authority → approval where required → execute via authorised connection → verify provider result → record evidence → learn.
