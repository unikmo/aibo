# Specialist B2B / Niche Expertise — add-on pack

**Activates on top of:** `Professional-Services` core pack. **Best for:** Pricing, M&A, regulatory, technology and other functional experts.

## Specific journey

Domain-specific trigger → research brief → technical diagnostic → scoped opinion/workplan → peer review → delivery → follow-up.

## Additional playbooks and templates

Technical research brief; evidence-quality checklist; expert positioning; high-trust sales sequence; client-facing technical framework.

## Additional KPIs

Qualified technical leads; discovery-to-proposal; win rate by niche; peer-review findings; repeat work. Use core metric definitions and report missing data honestly.

## Risks / approvals

Date/source technical claims; separate expert knowledge from regulated advice; require specialist/legal approval for financial, regulated or legal conclusions. All Soni / AIBO core approval gates remain controlling; the outreach delegation does not authorise publishing, contractual commitments, financial changes, confidential sharing or new geographies.

## Soni routing rule

Include this add-on's journey, additional templates, specialist evidence and specific risks in briefs and QA checklists. Use this add-on only when verified project services match it. Do not change the project's account permissions merely because the add-on is active.
