# Agency / Creative Services — add-on pack

**Activates on top of:** `Professional-Services` core pack. **Best for:** Marketing, design, branding, digital agencies.

## Specific journey

Client brief → research/strategy → concepts → collaborative feedback → production → approval → launch/report → case study.

## Additional playbooks and templates

Creative brief; campaign objectives and attribution; stakeholder matrix; revision-round policy; asset rights and handover checklist.

## Additional KPIs

Campaign goals and attribution; review turnaround; revisions vs contracted rounds; content performance; delivery margin. Use core metric definitions and report missing data honestly.

## Risks / approvals

Require client approval of creative and claim usage; approvals for publication/ad spend; third-party/licensed asset rights and testimonial releases. All Soni / AIBO core approval gates remain controlling; the outreach delegation does not authorise publishing, contractual commitments, financial changes, confidential sharing or new geographies.

## Soni routing rule

Include this add-on's journey, additional templates, specialist evidence and specific risks in briefs and QA checklists. Use this add-on only when verified project services match it. Do not change the project's account permissions merely because the add-on is active.
