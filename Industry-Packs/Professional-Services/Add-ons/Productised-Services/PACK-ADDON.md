# Productised Services — add-on pack

**Activates on top of:** `Professional-Services` core pack. **Best for:** Firms packaging repeatable work into bounded offers.

## Specific journey

Offer qualification → package selection → standard intake → repeatable delivery → acceptance → support/renewal.

## Additional playbooks and templates

Offer packaging and tier matrix; eligibility criteria; fixed scope and extras; pricing architecture; low-touch intake; SOP.

## Additional KPIs

Conversion by package; delivery time variance; gross margin; exceptions/upsells; support volume. Use core metric definitions and report missing data honestly.

## Risks / approvals

Approve prices, customer guarantees and contract terms; define exclusions and change-request routes; automation cannot silently increase commitments. All Soni / AIBO core approval gates remain controlling; the outreach delegation does not authorise publishing, contractual commitments, financial changes, confidential sharing or new geographies.

## Soni routing rule

Include this add-on's journey, additional templates, specialist evidence and specific risks in briefs and QA checklists. Use this add-on only when verified project services match it. Do not change the project's account permissions merely because the add-on is active.
