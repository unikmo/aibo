# Professional Services Edition — core pack

**Purpose:** For consultants, agencies, coaches, fractional executives and specialist B2B firms selling expertise, capacity and outcomes rather than on-site jobs. Use in conjunction with AIBO's shared operating core, not as a separate agent or parallel decision-maker.

## Activation

- Required: this core pack whenever a project is categorised as Professional Services.
- Optional: activate only the add-ons that match verified services and current engagement model. Multiple add-ons may be active, with the core's governance remaining authoritative.
- Read order: `AGENTS.md` → project facts → `System/CONNECTIONS.md` → this pack → relevant `01`–`05` modules → selected `Add-ons/*/PACK-ADDON.md` → relevant template.
- Do not load unrelated project/client context. Record activated add-ons and client-specific rules in the project's existing strategy/operating records, rather than creating duplicate project registries.

## Preflight questions (do not invent answers)

Services/offer, ICP/buyer, geographies and permissible prospecting methods; pricing model and approval authority; capacities and team; project/retainer/fractional engagement types; existing proof and authority assets; CRM, delivery and finance systems; contracts and data policies; measurable outcomes; channel permissions.

## Operating objective

Increase qualified pipeline, proposal speed and win quality; ensure reliable scope-bound delivery, profitable capacity, referrals and compounding reusable expertise. Prioritise evidence and economic quality over vanity traffic or message volume.

## Soni's responsibilities

Single client-facing partner: interpret priorities in terms of sales + capacity + outcomes; delegate research, growth, content, proposals, delivery and QA; require evidence-backed decision packages; respect confidentiality and commercial approval thresholds; record progress and metrics. Activation changes guidance and risk checks, never base authority or account permissions.

## Core modules

- `01-Customer-Journeys.md` — inbound, outbound/referral, project, retainer and fractional tracks.
- `02-Language-and-Scorecard.md` — terminology, positioning and metric definitions.
- `03-Playbooks-and-Templates.md` — applied workflows and reusable templates.
- `04-Connections-and-Approvals.md` — recommended systems, delegation and risks.
- `05-Routing-and-Activation.md` — how Soni composes modules and applies precedence.
- `Templates/` — ready-to-adapt working forms with validation/approval gates.

**Status:** These are documented operating instructions and templates, not proof of integrations, connected accounts, measured KPIs, or independently running specialist agents.
