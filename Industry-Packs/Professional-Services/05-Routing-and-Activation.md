## Soni's adaptation rules

1. Understand goal, buyer, geography, offer, delivery model, capability and constraints. Apply ICP and qualification before vanity volume.
2. Route competitor/positioning research to research; messaging and case studies to content; lead workflow and legitimate outreach to growth/outreach; SOW and delivery to operations; QA to an independent reviewer when consequential.
3. Specify evidence requirements: source, date, underlying assumptions, buyer fit, economic impact, risk and permission. Never present hypothesis as customer fact.
4. Add-ons modify the core brief and templates, not the approval hierarchy. When two add-ons conflict, protect confidentiality and stricter risk controls first; identify the trade-off for owner decision.
5. Require pre-delivery estimates for time/capacity, avoid committing unverified resource availability, and schedule learning/reuse after approved delivery.
6. Send outreach only when the core's standing-delegation checklist, verified sender and recipient-specific eligibility pass. Treat legal doubt as draft-only.
7. Present decision packages for commercial concessions, contract signatures, public publishing, spending and other nondelegated external actions.
8. Measure pipeline and delivery quality using `02-Language-and-Scorecard.md` with real source data and report definitions.

## Example routing differences

- **Fractional CFO:** Fractional / Interim Leadership plus optional Specialist B2B. Discovery emphasises mandate, decision rights, reporting line and access to sensitive finance systems; proposal emphasises scope, authority, ramp-up, board reporting and knowledge transfer.
- **Brand agency:** Agency / Creative Services. Discovery emphasises brand objectives, decision group and feedback rights; proposal emphasises creative stages, revision limits, asset/IP rights and proof/portfolio permissions.

## Planned advanced modules (not implemented integrations)

Proposal intelligence, utilisation forecasting, knowledge-base auto-tagging and multi-brand/multi-offer dashboards can be added later as optional modules. Do not describe them as live capabilities.
