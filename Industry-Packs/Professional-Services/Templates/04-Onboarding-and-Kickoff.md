# Onboarding / kickoff

- [ ] Signed scope and approved fees verified (no assumption of signature)
- [ ] Named client sponsor, delivery lead and escalation route
- [ ] Data processing, confidentiality and minimum necessary access cleared
- [ ] Kickoff agenda and baseline outcomes documented
- [ ] Timeline, milestones, dependencies and sign-off criteria agreed
- [ ] Project / retainer / fractional cadence selected
- [ ] Risk, change-request and reporting registers assigned
- [ ] Welcome note drafted; sending requires normal client-communication approval
