# Discovery call agenda and fit scorecard

**Agenda:** context and goal (5 min) → current state/constraints (10) → impact and urgency (10) → scope/buyers/decision process (10) → mutual next step (5). Adjust duration to circumstance.

Score 0–2 each (0 unknown/low, 1 partial, 2 verified): ICP fit; meaningful problem; buyer access; timing; budget/economic logic; delivery capability. Total /12; record rationale and source. A score is triage, not an automatic decision.
