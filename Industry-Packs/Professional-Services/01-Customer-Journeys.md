# Customer and service delivery journeys

| Stage | Goal | Soni / specialist deliverable | Exit signal | Approval gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | Traceable qualified demand | Source, ICP fit, need hypothesis, contact eligibility | Source and reason to engage recorded | Sending only under documented delegated outreach |
| Discovery / qualification | Understand business case, buyer, constraints | Discovery agenda, notes, BANT-like scorecard (without rigid disqualification), risks | Mutual fit, sponsor, timeline, next step | Avoid unauthorised promises |
| Proposal | Specify problem, outcome and feasibility | Proposal + scope of work (SOW), assumptions, fees, options | Decision-ready draft | Pricing/contract commitment approval |
| Negotiation | Resolve scope and economics | Redline register, concessions and trade-offs | Approved terms | Discounts and nonstandard terms gated |
| Onboarding | Establish governance and secure access | Welcome, stakeholders, RACI, milestones, systems permissions | Kickoff ready | Access/data permissions checked |
| Delivery | Meet agreed outcomes on time and margin | Milestone plan, status reports, deliverable QA, change log | Acceptance evidence | Scope changes gated |
| Review / proof | Learn and substantiate outcomes | Results review, approved case-study draft | Client sign-off where needed | Public claims/testimonials gated |
| Expansion / referral / renewal | Retain trust and improve account value | QBR, follow-up offer, referral ask draft | Recorded next action | Offers/commitments gated |

## Acquisition path branching

- **Inbound thought-leadership:** asset/topic → attribution → qualification → discovery → proposal. Capture content-to-lead conversion, source and buyer intent; do not assume site traffic equals demand.
- **Outbound research/referral:** establish relevance and lawful contact eligibility → tailored first touch → interest handling → discovery. Referral introduction must be genuine; do not imply personal endorsement without proof. Respect `AGENTS.md` outreach delegation and stop conditions.

## Engagement models

- **Project:** fixed objective → defined scope/milestones/acceptance → delivery → closure and case study. Monitor change requests, scope creep, fixed-fee effective rate and delivery margin.
- **Retainer:** baseline + monthly capacity/SLA → recurring work cadence → monthly utilisation/value report → quarterly business review → renewal or expansion. Monitor renewals, out-of-scope requests and unused/overused allocations.
- **Fractional/interim executive:** agreed authority and outcomes → stakeholder discovery → 30/60/90-day ramp-up → operating rhythm and decision governance → knowledge transfer and exit/succession. Never imply executive authority, signing rights or fiduciary role without contract verification.
