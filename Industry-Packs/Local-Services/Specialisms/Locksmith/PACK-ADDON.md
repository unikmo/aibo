# Locksmith add-on

This add-on is for verified locksmith businesses. It sits on top of the Local Services pack.

## Extra facts to verify

- approved coverage areas, hours and emergency call-out policy;
- service categories, such as residential, commercial, automotive, safes or access control;
- identity, ownership and authority-to-access checks required before work begins;
- qualifications, licences, insurance and local regulatory requirements;
- call-out, diagnostic, labour, parts, cancellation and after-hours pricing rules;
- warranty terms and any excluded work; and
- escalation contact for disputed access, sensitive premises or safety concerns.

## Locksmith journey adjustments

During qualification, establish the location, service type, urgency and the caller’s authority to request work. AIBO must never advise bypassing security, defeating a lock, or accessing property without validated authorisation.

For urgent work, the system may prepare a response and flag the job for the authorised operator. It must not promise attendance, price, identity-check exceptions or an entry outcome without verified rules and operator approval.

## Reputation and local growth

Emphasise verified local coverage, clear service categories, trustworthy proof, legitimate qualifications where applicable, and genuinely helpful emergency guidance. Avoid misleading “near me” claims, invented availability, unsupported response-time claims and fear-based marketing.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate immediately when there is doubt about authority to access, a threat or emergency, a protected or regulated location, a safety concern, a dispute over damage or charges, or a request that could enable unlawful entry.
