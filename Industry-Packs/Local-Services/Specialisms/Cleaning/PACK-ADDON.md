# Cleaning add-on

This add-on is for verified residential, commercial, move-out, specialist or recurring cleaning businesses. It sits on top of the Local Services pack.

## Extra facts to verify

- service area, hours, service categories and exclusions;
- recurring, one-off, move-in/out, commercial, deep-clean or specialist-service rules;
- staff vetting, insurance, access, key, alarm, privacy and property-care procedures;
- chemical, allergy, pet, food-area and sensitive-environment policies;
- pricing, minimums, cancellation, satisfaction, damage and refund rules; and
- who may authorise bookings, access arrangements, recurring plans, refunds or scope changes.

## Cleaning journey adjustments

Capture service type, property type, size, timing, access, pets, sensitivities, requested extras and any restricted areas. AIBO can draft a scope and confirmation. It must not assure a result, promise specific staff, accept keys or codes, confirm price or alter a booking without verified rules and approval.

Trust, respect for the home or workplace, clear scope and reliable recurring service are the central growth messages. Avoid unsupported “guaranteed” claims, misleading staff credentials or public discussion of client premises.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate key, alarm or access-code issues; suspected theft, loss or property damage; hazardous materials; medical or biohazard concerns; privacy complaints; worker safety issues; disputes about scope, quality or refunds; and requests for unsafe cleaning guidance.
