# Electrical add-on

This add-on is for verified electrical businesses. It sits on top of the Local Services pack.

## Extra facts to verify

- service area, operating hours and emergency policy;
- supported residential, commercial, installation, inspection, repair and upgrade services;
- current licences, registrations, insurance, certifications, permit and inspection requirements;
- rules for diagnostics, call-outs, repairs, installations, after-hours work, quotations and warranties;
- any work categories the business is not authorised or equipped to perform; and
- who may approve urgent dispatch, electrical-work quotes, scope changes or pricing exceptions.

## Electrical journey adjustments

Record the service type, reported symptom, property type, urgency, any immediate danger signal and relevant access information. AIBO can prepare a response, qualification questions and an internal brief. It must not diagnose, instruct a customer to work on an electrical system, promise compliance, quote a job or confirm attendance without verified rules and approval.

Growth activity must use substantiated credentials, service areas and capabilities. Electrical safety, licensing and code-compliance claims require owner validation before publication.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate reports of smoke, sparks, burning smells, exposed wiring, electric shock, repeated power loss, water near electrical equipment, fire damage, urgent safety risk, disputed compliance, insurance claims or requests for instructions that could expose someone to harm.
