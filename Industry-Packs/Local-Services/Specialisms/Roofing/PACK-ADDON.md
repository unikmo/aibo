# Roofing add-on

This add-on is for verified roofing businesses. It sits on top of the Local Services pack.

## Extra facts to verify

- service area, emergency and weather-related availability;
- roofing systems, inspection, repair, replacement and maintenance services offered;
- licences, insurance, safety credentials, permit and inspection requirements;
- inspection, estimate, deposit, change-order, warranty and cancellation rules; and
- who may approve an on-site visit, emergency response, claim-related communication or scope change.

## Roofing journey adjustments

Record roof type where known, the reported problem, visible damage, property type, urgency, weather exposure, access constraints and whether insurance is involved. AIBO can prepare qualification questions, internal summaries and estimate drafts; it must not diagnose damage remotely, promise weatherproofing, confirm insurance coverage or commit price and schedule without approved rules.

Marketing must use evidence-backed local coverage, credentials, work examples and warranty language. Avoid fear-based claims, fabricated storm urgency, unsupported damage assertions or suggestions that a claim will be accepted.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate active leaks, structural concerns, fallen material, unsafe access, extreme-weather exposure, disputes about damage or workmanship, insurance claims, deposit disputes, permit issues and requests for unsafe repair guidance.
