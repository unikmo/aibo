# Pest Control add-on

This add-on is for verified pest-control businesses. It sits on top of the Local Services pack.

## Extra facts to verify

- service area, target pest categories and work the business does not offer;
- licences, certifications, insurance and applicable treatment regulations;
- inspection, treatment, follow-up, recurrence, guarantee and cancellation rules;
- household, pet, food-preparation, allergy and vulnerable-occupant precautions approved by the business; and
- who may approve treatment bookings, recurring plans, property-manager work or price exceptions.

## Pest-control journey adjustments

Capture the reported pest, location, property type, extent, urgency, occupant considerations and access constraints. AIBO can ask approved qualification questions and prepare a visit brief. It must not identify pests as certain, prescribe treatment, make safety assurances, promise eradication or give chemical-use instructions without verified business information and owner approval.

Growth materials should focus on genuine service areas, qualified help, prevention content approved by the business and clear expectations. Do not use stigma, panic, unverified infestation claims or unsupported guarantees.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate concerns involving toxic exposure, bites or illness, protected wildlife, tenant or landlord disputes, food premises, schools or care settings, chemical sensitivity, treatment failure claims, regulatory complaints or requests for unsafe treatment instructions.
