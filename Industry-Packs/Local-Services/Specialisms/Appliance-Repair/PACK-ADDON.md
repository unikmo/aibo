# Appliance Repair add-on

This add-on is for verified appliance-repair and maintenance businesses. It sits on top of the Local Services pack.

## Extra facts to verify

- service area, operating hours and supported appliance brands, types and fuel sources;
- diagnostic, repair, maintenance, parts, installation and exclusion rules;
- licences, insurance, manufacturer credentials, warranty and safety requirements;
- call-out, labour, parts, after-hours, cancellation, repair-authorisation and payment rules; and
- who may approve a visit, parts order, replacement option, warranty communication or price exception.

## Appliance-repair journey adjustments

Capture appliance type, brand where known, reported symptom, age where available, property type, urgency, fuel or power source, warranty status and access conditions. AIBO can draft qualification questions and an internal work brief. It must not diagnose remotely, tell a customer to dismantle or repair equipment, guarantee a part, commit to warranty coverage or quote without verified rules.

Marketing should use supported categories, verified credentials, realistic turnaround information and clear service limits. Do not claim universal brand support, guaranteed repair or manufacturer authorisation without evidence.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate gas smells, smoke, burning, electric shock, water leaks near power, food-safety or refrigeration-loss concerns, warranty disputes, property damage, requests for unsafe repair instructions and issues involving vulnerable occupants.
