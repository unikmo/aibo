# Restoration and Water Damage add-on

This add-on is for verified restoration, flood-response, drying, fire-recovery or water-damage businesses. It sits on top of the Local Services pack.

## Extra facts to verify

- service area, response hours, emergency policy and service capabilities;
- work categories offered, including water, fire, mould, contents, drying or reconstruction, and exclusions;
- licences, insurance, contamination, safety, disposal and regulatory requirements;
- inspection, documentation, insurer, deposit, scope-change and payment rules; and
- who may approve emergency attendance, insurer communication, change orders or public statements.

## Restoration journey adjustments

Record the incident type, timing, source where known, property type, affected areas, possible contamination, safety risk, occupant needs, insurer involvement and access information. AIBO may create an internal incident brief and communication draft. It must not classify contamination, guarantee restoration, state insurance coverage, advise unsafe clean-up or promise attendance or price without verified rules and approval.

Use factual recovery information and verified proof of service. Do not exploit distress, overstate damage, claim insurer approval or publish private incident details.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate active flooding, electrical or structural danger, sewage or hazardous-material concerns, mould or air-quality claims, fire damage, vulnerable occupants, insurer disputes, evidence-preservation issues, disputed invoices and requests for unsafe recovery instructions.
