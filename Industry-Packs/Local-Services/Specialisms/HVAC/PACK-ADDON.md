# HVAC add-on

This add-on is for verified heating, ventilation and air-conditioning businesses. It sits on top of the Local Services pack.

## Extra facts to verify

- service area, operating hours and emergency policy;
- systems supported, including heating, cooling, ventilation and heat-pump work;
- approved fuel types, refrigerant-service scope, qualifications, licences and insurance;
- diagnostic, call-out, repair, maintenance, installation and after-hours pricing rules;
- permit, inspection, manufacturer-warranty and maintenance-plan requirements; and
- who may approve replacements, discounts, finance options or urgent dispatch.

## HVAC journey adjustments

During qualification, record the system type, reported issue, property type, urgency, safety signals and availability. AIBO may draft a response and prepare a job brief, but it must not diagnose remotely, promise a repair, quote an installation or confirm attendance without verified business rules.

Use seasonal demand, preventative maintenance, energy efficiency and verified local trust signals to shape planning. Claims about savings, response times, qualifications, availability or rebates need evidence and owner approval.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate reports of gas odour, possible carbon-monoxide exposure, smoke, burning smells, repeated electrical trips, suspected refrigerant release, vulnerable occupants without critical heating or cooling, property damage, disputes, or regulatory and warranty claims. Do not give technical repair instructions to a customer.
