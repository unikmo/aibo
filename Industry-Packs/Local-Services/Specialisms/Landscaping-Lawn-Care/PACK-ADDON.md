# Landscaping and Lawn Care add-on

This add-on is for verified landscaping, lawn-care, garden-maintenance, irrigation or outdoor-maintenance businesses. It sits on top of the Local Services pack.

## Extra facts to verify

- service area, seasons, weather restrictions and supported service categories;
- recurring, one-off, design, installation, tree, irrigation or maintenance scope and exclusions;
- licences, insurance, pesticide, irrigation, tree-work, disposal and permit requirements where relevant;
- site-visit, estimate, deposit, change-order, maintenance-plan and cancellation rules; and
- who may approve schedule changes, plant or material substitutions, recurring plans or price exceptions.

## Landscaping journey adjustments

Capture property type, requested service, site size, condition, access, timing, seasonal factors, water constraints and potential hazards. AIBO may prepare a visit brief and estimate draft. It must not promise plant survival, drainage outcomes, permit approval, weather-dependent dates or unverified pricing.

Marketing should use genuine local climate knowledge, verified work examples, seasonal timing and clear service limits. Do not use altered before-and-after evidence, invented availability or unsupported environmental claims.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate tree or site hazards, utility-location concerns, pesticide or chemical questions, irrigation leaks, property damage, boundary disputes, protected-land or permit issues, weather emergencies and injury or insurance claims.
