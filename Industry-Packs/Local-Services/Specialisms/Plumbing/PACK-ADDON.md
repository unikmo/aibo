# Plumbing add-on

This add-on is for verified plumbing businesses. It sits on top of the Local Services pack.

## Extra facts to verify

- service area, operating hours and emergency policy;
- supported work such as repairs, drainage, fixtures, water heating, commercial plumbing or gas work where legally permitted;
- licences, insurance, permit and inspection requirements;
- call-out, diagnostic, labour, parts, after-hours, cancellation and warranty rules;
- exclusions, including work the business does not undertake; and
- who may authorise urgent dispatch, discounts, work-scope changes or replacement options.

## Plumbing journey adjustments

Capture the location of the issue, visible symptoms, water or drainage impact, urgency, access constraints and any safety flags. AIBO must not diagnose from a message, promise a fix, approve a price or make a customer-facing booking change without verified rules and the agreed approval route.

Marketing and local-content work should focus on legitimate service coverage, clear service categories, preventative advice approved by the business, proof of competence and customer trust. Avoid false urgency, unsupported “same-day” claims and invented pricing.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate major or ongoing water damage, wastewater or contamination concerns, possible gas issues, loss of essential water service for vulnerable occupants, unsafe access, property-management disputes, insurance-related claims, disputed charges, or any request for technical instructions that could increase risk.
