# Home Maintenance and Handyman add-on

This add-on is for verified home-maintenance, property-maintenance and handyman businesses. It sits on top of the Local Services pack.

## Extra facts to verify

- service area, operating hours, supported tasks and explicit exclusions;
- the boundary between general maintenance and regulated specialist work;
- licences, insurance, permits, qualifications and subcontractor rules;
- site-visit, estimate, hourly, minimum-charge, materials, change-order, deposit and warranty rules; and
- who may approve scope, substitutions, extra work, schedule changes, discounts or refunds.

## Home-maintenance journey adjustments

Capture the requested tasks, property type, access needs, photos or evidence if provided, timing, materials expectations, relevant hazards and whether regulated work may be involved. AIBO can prepare a scope checklist and estimate draft. It must not confirm that work is permitted, diagnose structural or technical conditions, accept an open-ended task list or promise a final price without verified scope and owner approval.

Marketing must make the scope and limits clear. Do not imply that one provider is licensed for every trade, conceal exclusions or use vague “we do everything” promises.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate structural concerns, electrical, gas or plumbing issues beyond verified authorisation, asbestos or hazardous-material concerns, unsafe access, property damage, landlord-tenant disputes, scope disputes, permit questions and requests for unsafe DIY instructions.
