# Garage Doors add-on

This add-on is for verified garage-door, opener and access-system businesses. It sits on top of the Local Services pack.

## Extra facts to verify

- service area, emergency availability and supported door, opener and access-system types;
- repair, installation, maintenance, replacement and commercial-service scope;
- licences, insurance, manufacturer credentials, warranty and permit requirements;
- diagnostic, call-out, parts, labour, after-hours and cancellation rules; and
- who may approve urgent jobs, price changes, replacement options or access-related communications.

## Garage-door journey adjustments

Capture the reported fault, door or opener type, property type, urgency, access impact, safety signals and availability. AIBO can prepare questions and a job brief. It must not give mechanical repair instructions, promise access or safety outcomes, quote unverified parts or confirm an appointment without authorised business rules.

Local growth should use substantiated expertise, real service categories and clear availability. Avoid implying guaranteed emergency arrival, unrestricted property access or universal compatibility.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate trapped-person or vehicle concerns, unstable or damaged doors, broken components that create safety risks, suspected forced entry, commercial-security issues, property damage, warranty disputes and requests for unsafe repair steps.
