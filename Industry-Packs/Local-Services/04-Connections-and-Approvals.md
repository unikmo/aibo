# Local Services connections and approvals

Connections are managed centrally in `System/CONNECTIONS.md`. This pack only defines the usual priorities and safeguards for this industry.

## Typical connection priorities

1. Customer enquiries and CRM
2. Scheduling or field-service system
3. Quoting and invoicing system
4. Business listings and review profiles
5. Website analytics and call tracking
6. Advertising platforms

## Permission boundaries

| Activity | Default permission |
| --- | --- |
| Read connected data and prepare an internal analysis | Allowed where the connection grants it |
| Draft a reply, quote, review response or campaign | Allowed as a draft only |
| Send a message, publish a post or respond publicly | Explicit owner approval unless a documented pre-approval exists |
| Create or amend a booking, quote, invoice, payment request or refund | Explicit owner approval |
| Launch, alter or fund advertising | Explicit owner approval |
| Change access, customer records, prices or integrations | Explicit owner approval |

## Risk flags

Immediately escalate claims concerning safety, injury, damage, access/security, discrimination, disputed charges, regulatory requirements, warranties, personal data, urgent outages or threats of legal action.
