# Local Services playbooks

## Enquiry response draft

Use verified business facts only.

1. Acknowledge the issue and the customer’s stated urgency.
2. Confirm the service area and ask only the questions needed to assess fit.
3. Explain the next step without promising a price, arrival time or outcome that is not approved.
4. Offer the approved contact or booking route.
5. Record the lead source and any safety, access or urgency concern.

## Quote follow-up

1. Refer to the approved quote and the date sent.
2. Ask whether any question is holding up the decision.
3. Restate the verified next step and expiry or availability rule, if one exists.
4. Do not introduce a discount, guarantee or scope change without authorisation.

## Review request

1. Send only after confirmed satisfactory completion and the owner’s agreed timing rule.
2. Keep the request short and neutral; never pressure or incentivise a positive review where rules prohibit it.
3. Use the approved review destination.
4. Escalate a complaint or poor outcome privately instead of seeking a public review.

## Local visibility review

Check the business name, contact details, service area, opening information, category, key services, current reviews, photos and primary local listings. Identify inconsistencies, missing evidence and competitor patterns before proposing changes.
