# Local Services scorecard

The Soni selects a small starting scorecard with the owner. Metrics are useful only when their source and definition are clear.

## Demand and response

| Measure | Definition | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified leads | Enquiries that fit the service area, service and basic requirements | Separates real demand from noise |
| First-response time | Time from enquiry to the first useful reply | Often determines who wins urgent local work |
| Lead source | Where each qualified enquiry originated | Shows which channels earn investment |
| Booking rate | Qualified leads that become booked work | Reveals sales and scheduling friction |

## Job economics

| Measure | Definition | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quote acceptance rate | Accepted quotes divided by quotes sent | Tests offer clarity and pricing position |
| Average job value | Revenue divided by completed jobs | Tracks quality of work mix |
| Gross-margin view | Revenue less directly attributable delivery costs, when reliable data exists | Protects profitable growth |
| Cancellation or no-show rate | Lost booked work divided by bookings | Highlights confirmation and qualification problems |

## Trust and repeat business

| Measure | Definition | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review request rate | Eligible completed jobs offered a review request | Makes reputation building consistent |
| Review quality | Rating, themes and unresolved concerns | Exposes service and trust issues |
| Repeat or referral work | Work from prior customers or referred customers | Shows durable local value |

## Target setting

Finance validates targets after market research. Start with low, base and high cases, show the assumptions, and distinguish forecast from fact. Do not promise revenue from unverified traffic or conversion assumptions.
