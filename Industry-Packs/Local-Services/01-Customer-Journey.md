# Local service customer journey

This is the default journey. A specialism may add steps but must not remove owner approval requirements.

| Stage | Desired outcome | AIBO focus | Owner approval needed? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | A local prospect finds a clear, credible offer | Search visibility, listings, reviews, social proof and paid-media planning | Before ad spend or publishing unless pre-approved |
| Enquire | The right enquiry is captured quickly | Clear contact routes, response drafts, qualification questions and source tracking | No, for internal draft work |
| Qualify | Scope, location, urgency and feasibility are known | Check service area, problem, timing, access, budget signals and risk flags | Before promising price, availability or outcome |
| Quote or estimate | Customer receives a clear, accurate next step | Draft scope, exclusions, options, payment terms and follow-up | Yes, unless the owner has approved an exact pricing rule |
| Book and prepare | Correct person, time and materials are ready | Confirm appointment, reminders, work brief and hand-off | Yes for booking changes that affect the customer |
| Deliver | Work is safely completed and recorded | Completion checklist, evidence capture and issue escalation | Owner or authorised operator controls the work |
| Invoice and collect | Correct invoice is issued and payment is tracked | Draft invoice notes, payment reminder sequence and overdue view | Yes before sending or changing money-related records |
| Review and retain | Satisfied customers review, return or refer | Request draft, review response draft, maintenance reminder and referral plan | Yes before public posting or outreach unless pre-approved |

## Non-negotiable controls

- Never confirm a service, price, appointment, licence, warranty or completion result without verified business rules.
- Do not contact a customer, publish content, spend money, alter a booking, issue an invoice or access a payment system without the agreed approval route.
- Treat emergency, safety, access, security and personal-data issues as priority escalation items.
