# Local Services pack

Use this pack for businesses whose customers search locally, request a service, receive a quote or estimate, book a visit, and expect the work to be completed at a home or premises. It supports trades, repairs, maintenance and mobile services.

It complements the AIBO core. The core manages research, finances, planning, delivery quality, risk, marketing, reporting and approval. This pack makes those steps fit a local service business.

## Operating aim

Turn a good local enquiry into a well-qualified booking, a completed job, a correctly issued invoice, a positive review, and—where appropriate—repeat or referral work.

## What must be known before action

- service area, operating hours and emergency availability;
- services offered, exclusions, fees and pricing method;
- licences, insurance, certifications and safety requirements;
- target customer types and priority job types;
- who may quote, discount, schedule, refund or approve spend;
- current website, business listings, review profiles and lead sources; and
- systems used for enquiries, dispatch, quotes, invoices, payments and customer records.

Unknown facts must be marked as needing owner confirmation. AIBO must not invent prices, availability, qualifications or service coverage.

## Typical work types

- market, competitor and service-area research;
- local landing pages, service pages and helpful content;
- quote, follow-up, review-request and reactivation drafts;
- lead handling and booking-process improvement;
- paid-media planning and reporting;
- review-response drafts and reputation monitoring; and
- operating dashboards for conversion, response time and job economics.
