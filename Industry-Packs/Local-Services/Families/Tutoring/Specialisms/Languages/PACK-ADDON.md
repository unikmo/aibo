# Languages add-on

Use this add-on with the Tutoring family for verified language-tuition businesses.

## Extra facts to verify

- languages, learner levels, age groups, lesson formats and teaching availability;
- tutor capability, language-pair limits, qualifications and safeguarding requirements;
- placement, lesson, practice, assessment, recording and communication policies;
- package, cancellation, payment, refund and progress-reporting rules; and
- who may confirm enrolment, assign a tutor, discuss learner information or alter terms.

## Operating adjustments

Capture the learner’s target language, current level if known, stated goal, schedule and preferred format. For minors, follow the verified guardian-contact process. AIBO may prepare an enquiry response, intake checklist and learning-plan outline. It must not guarantee fluency, test scores, visa, employment or academic outcomes; assess a learner beyond verified processes; disclose information; or confirm tuition terms without approval.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate safeguarding concerns, inappropriate contact, learner-data or recording issues, discrimination complaints, disputes over outcome claims, payment disputes, welfare concerns and any request to misrepresent language credentials or assessment results.
