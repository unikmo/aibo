# Tutoring family

Use this family for verified local or hybrid tutoring businesses. It supports future add-ons such as school subjects, test preparation, languages and music tuition.

## What must be verified

- subjects, levels, age groups, teaching format, locations and availability;
- tutor qualifications, background-check, safeguarding, insurance and local requirements;
- guardian contact, consent, learner-data, recording and communication policies;
- assessment, lesson, homework, cancellation, payment, package and refund rules;
- realistic learning-outcome and exam-result claim boundaries; and
- who may confirm enrolment, assign a tutor, change a schedule, discuss learner information or approve a refund.

## Family operating model

Capture the learner’s requested subject, level, goals, schedule and format. For a minor, use the verified guardian-contact and consent process. AIBO may prepare an enquiry response, intake checklist, lesson-plan outline and progress-summary draft. It must not guarantee grades or exam results, make educational or medical assessments, disclose learner information, arrange unsupervised contact or confirm tuition terms without verified rules and approval.

Growth work should make the teaching approach, tutor credibility, learner level and expected commitment clear. Avoid pressure on parents, fabricated results, false credentials and promises of admissions or grade outcomes.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate safeguarding concerns, a minor contacting outside the approved process, harassment or inappropriate communication, data-privacy issues, discrimination complaints, disputes over educational claims, payment disputes and any concern about learner welfare.
