# Health and Medical Services family

Use this US-oriented family for verified health-service organisations that operate locally or regionally. It supports Dentistry, Physician Practices, ABA Providers, Mental Health and Counselling, Physical and Occupational Rehabilitation, and Pharmacies.

This is an operational pack, not clinical or legal advice and not a HIPAA certification. It helps AIBO keep operational work separate from patient information unless a practice deliberately approves a suitable patient-data workflow.

## Two mailbox and data modes

Mailbox capability is available, but no mailbox, EHR, patient portal, patient CRM or attachment source is connected at initial setup.

| Mode | AIBO may use | AIBO must not use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operations-only | Owner-supplied aggregate measures, staffing and capacity data, non-patient supplier/admin work, public market research and internal planning | Patient messages, names, contact details, medical information, appointments, records, attachments, EHR or portal data |
| HIPAA Mailroom | The specifically approved patient mailbox workflow for prioritisation and drafts, within the practice’s approved data path | Clinical advice, triage, diagnosis, treatment decisions, autonomous replies, unapproved storage or onward sharing |

Before HIPAA Mailroom is enabled, the practice must confirm its authorised mailbox, purpose, access scope, approved service providers and contractual/privacy requirements, retention and audit expectations, and responsible owner. Where a business associate agreement is required, it must be in place. All outgoing patient communication remains owner-approved.

## Family operating model

Improve scheduling, capacity, referral flow, utilisation, revenue-cycle coordination, staff workload, public-facing communication and non-clinical operations. Do not make clinical decisions, provide emergency guidance, diagnose, prescribe, triage or replace professional judgement.

## Common measures

- appointment capacity and fill rate;
- cancellation and no-show rate;
- days to next available appointment;
- referral volume and closure rate;
- authorised versus delivered services, where relevant;
- staff utilisation and administrative workload; and
- patient-service measures only when the practice has approved an appropriate data workflow.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate any clinical, emergency, patient-safety, safeguarding, privacy, security, billing, insurer, regulatory, discrimination, consent or record-access concern to the authorised practice person. Do not treat a policy document as proof that the live configuration is compliant.
