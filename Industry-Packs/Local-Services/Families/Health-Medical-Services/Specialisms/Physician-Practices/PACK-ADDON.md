# Physician Practices add-on

Use this module with the Health and Medical Services family for verified outpatient physician practices. A specialty may later need its own module.

## Operational focus

Support capacity planning, appointment flow, referral coordination, administrative tasks, staffing, non-clinical communication, public information and performance reporting.

## Facts and measures to verify

- specialties, locations, hours, provider roles, patient-service boundaries and payer model;
- referral, appointment, cancellation, wait-list, payment and records-request workflows;
- public statements, provider biography, credential and testimonial approval rules;
- days to appointment, schedule fill rate, no-show rate, referral closure, staff workload and administrative turnaround measures; and
- who may approve scheduling, public content, patient-message drafts or financial actions.

## Boundaries and escalation

AIBO must not diagnose, triage, prescribe, give emergency advice, interpret symptoms, alter medical records or communicate patient-specific clinical information without the approved HIPAA Mailroom workflow and owner approval. Escalate clinical, privacy, consent, safety, insurer, billing and complaint matters to authorised staff.
