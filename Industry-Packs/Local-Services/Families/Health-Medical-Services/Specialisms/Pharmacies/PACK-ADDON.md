# Pharmacies add-on

Use this module with the Health and Medical Services family for verified community, retail or local pharmacy businesses. Pharmacy is deliberately included because its operational model differs from a physician practice while it shares high privacy and regulatory stakes.

## Operational focus

Support staffing, public service information, inventory and supplier administration, non-clinical workflow, refill-process administration, delivery or collection operations and approved local marketing.

## Facts and measures to verify

- pharmacy type, locations, hours, services, delivery/collection area and applicable licences;
- dispensing, refill, controlled-substance, consultation, identity, payment, delivery and records policies;
- supplier, inventory, shortage, staffing, payer and public-communications workflow;
- waiting-time, refill-process turnaround, inventory availability, staffing load, delivery/collection performance and customer-service measures; and
- who may approve patient communication drafts, inventory actions, public claims, financial actions or workflow changes.

## Boundaries and escalation

AIBO must not dispense, recommend, substitute, dose, assess interactions, give medication advice, access prescription or patient data outside an approved HIPAA workflow, or send patient-specific communication without approval. Escalate medication, safety, privacy, controlled-substance, regulatory, payer, complaint and clinical questions to an authorised pharmacist or practice lead.
