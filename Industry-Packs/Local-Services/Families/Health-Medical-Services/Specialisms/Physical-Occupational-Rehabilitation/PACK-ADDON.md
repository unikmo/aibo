# Physical and Occupational Rehabilitation add-on

Use this module with the Health and Medical Services family for verified physical therapy, occupational therapy and outpatient rehabilitation clinics.

## Operational focus

Support referral and intake administration, evaluation scheduling, plan-of-care workflow coordination, authorised-visit tracking, session utilisation, staffing and non-clinical follow-up.

## Facts and measures to verify

- services, locations, practitioner roles, payer mix and referral requirements;
- intake, plan-of-care, authorisation, scheduling, cancellation, documentation, billing and discharge administration rules;
- approved public claims, outcomes language and testimonial rules;
- referral closure, evaluation-to-treatment conversion, authorised versus delivered visits, utilisation, cancellation/no-show and capacity measures; and
- who may approve schedule, patient communication draft, public content, billing or workflow changes.

## Boundaries and escalation

AIBO must not assess a patient, provide exercise or treatment guidance, alter a plan of care, interpret clinical documentation, promise recovery outcomes or send patient-specific clinical communication without approved scope and owner approval. Escalate injury, safety, consent, privacy, payer, records, complaint and clinical matters to authorised staff.
