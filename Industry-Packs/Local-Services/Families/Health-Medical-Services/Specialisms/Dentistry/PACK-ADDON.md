# Dentistry add-on

Use this module with the Health and Medical Services family for verified dental practices.

## Operational focus

Support appointment capacity, hygiene recall administration, treatment-plan acceptance workflow, referral and lab coordination, public reputation, staffing and non-clinical practice operations.

## Facts and measures to verify

- services, locations, providers, hours, insurance and self-pay boundaries;
- recall, consultation, treatment-plan, cancellation, deposit and payment policies;
- approved public claims, imagery, testimonials and before-and-after consent rules;
- treatment-plan acceptance, hygiene recall completion, cancellation/no-show, capacity and days-to-appointment measures; and
- who may approve schedules, patient communication drafts, pricing or financial arrangements.

## Boundaries and escalation

AIBO must not provide dental advice, assess a patient, discuss treatment details outside an approved patient-data mode, promise clinical outcomes, alter a record or send a patient message without approval. Escalate pain, infection, emergency, consent, privacy, complaint, billing or clinical questions to authorised practice staff.
