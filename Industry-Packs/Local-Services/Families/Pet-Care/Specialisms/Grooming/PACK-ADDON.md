# Pet Grooming add-on

Use this add-on with the Pet Care family for verified pet-grooming businesses.

## Extra facts to verify

- animal types, breeds, sizes, coat conditions and services accepted or excluded;
- grooming, bathing, clipping, de-matting, hygiene and handling boundaries;
- intake, vaccination, behaviour, allergy, welfare, emergency and veterinary-referral policies;
- pricing, condition-based scope changes, cancellation, late pick-up, damage and refund rules; and
- who may accept a pet, approve a scope or price change, contact an emergency provider or issue a refund.

## Operating adjustments

Capture the pet type, requested service, owner-provided care needs, appointment timing, behaviour information, condition notes and emergency contact. AIBO may prepare an intake checklist and booking draft. It must not judge an animal fit for grooming, diagnose a condition, approve sedation or medication, promise a grooming outcome or accept a pet without verified rules and approval.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate illness, injury, animal distress, aggressive behaviour, skin or coat concerns needing professional assessment, missing or escaped animals, welfare complaints, owner-authority disputes, payment disputes and requests for veterinary advice.
