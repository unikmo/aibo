# Pet Care family

Use this family for verified non-veterinary pet-service businesses. It supports future add-ons such as grooming, walking, sitting, boarding and training. Veterinary diagnosis and treatment need a distinct, regulated healthcare pack and are outside this family unless separately built.

## What must be verified

- animal types, services offered, service area, opening hours and capacity limits;
- owner-authority, intake, vaccination, behaviour, medication and emergency-contact policies;
- staff training, insurance, animal-welfare, transport, boarding and local licensing requirements;
- booking, cancellation, deposits, recurring-service, late-pick-up and refund rules;
- data, photo, location-sharing and access-to-home policies; and
- who may confirm a booking, accept a pet, change care arrangements, contact an emergency provider or approve a refund.

## Family operating model

Capture the requested service, pet type, relevant care needs provided by the owner, timing, location, access constraints and emergency contact. AIBO can prepare an intake checklist, care brief and customer-message draft. It must not give veterinary advice, assess an animal as safe, promise a behavioural outcome, make medication decisions, accept an animal or confirm a booking without verified rules and approval.

Trust, consistent care, welfare, clear boundaries and owner communication are the central growth themes. Do not use misleading animal transformations, publicly share pet locations or imply veterinary expertise without evidence.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate illness, injury, missing or escaped animals, aggressive behaviour, suspected neglect or abuse, medication concerns, owner-authority disputes, property-access concerns, transport incidents, welfare complaints and any request for veterinary advice.
