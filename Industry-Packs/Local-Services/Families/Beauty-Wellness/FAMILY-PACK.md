# Beauty and Wellness family

Use this family for verified appointment-led personal-care and wellness businesses. It supports future add-ons such as hair, nails, aesthetics, massage, fitness and other clearly defined services.

## What must be verified

- services offered, client eligibility, age, consent and health-screening rules;
- staff qualifications, licences, insurance, hygiene, sanitation and local regulatory requirements;
- appointment duration, locations, deposits, cancellation, no-show, refund and membership rules;
- privacy, photography, testimonial and client-record policies;
- product, allergy, patch-test and aftercare boundaries approved by the business; and
- who may approve bookings, discounts, client messages, refunds, published images or service changes.

## Family operating model

Capture the requested service, appointment preference, client eligibility information only where necessary, consent needs, sensitivities disclosed by the client and any booking constraints. AIBO may prepare approved booking and aftercare-message drafts. It must not provide medical advice, diagnose a condition, assure an outcome, collect unnecessary sensitive information, publish client images or confirm an appointment, price or treatment without verified rules and approval.

Growth work should be distinctive and creative while remaining truthful: verified work examples, clear service descriptions, consented client stories and realistic expectations. Avoid body-shaming, unsupported health or outcome claims, misleading edits and pressure-based marketing.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate adverse reactions, illness or injury concerns, consent or privacy complaints, issues involving minors, harassment or discrimination concerns, hygiene complaints, disputed charges, requests for medical advice and any regulatory complaint.
