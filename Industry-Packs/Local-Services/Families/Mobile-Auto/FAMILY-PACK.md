# Mobile Auto family

Use this family for verified businesses that provide vehicle services at a customer location, roadside or another agreed site. It supports future add-ons such as mobile mechanic, detailing, tyres, roadside assistance and vehicle diagnostics.

## What must be verified

- service area, operating hours, roadside or emergency policy and supported vehicle types;
- services offered, exclusions, parts-supply rules, diagnostic limits and warranty terms;
- licences, insurance, roadside-safety requirements and towing or recovery boundaries where relevant;
- call-out, diagnostic, labour, parts, travel, cancellation and after-hours pricing rules;
- safe site, vehicle-access, key-handling and vehicle-custody policies; and
- who may approve dispatch, parts orders, repair scope, price changes, refunds or customer updates.

## Family operating model

Capture the vehicle type, reported issue, location, whether the vehicle is safely situated, urgency, access constraints, warranty context and customer contact details. AIBO can prepare qualification questions, an internal brief and approved message drafts. It must not diagnose a fault, advise unsafe roadside work, promise a repair, order parts, accept custody of a vehicle or confirm a price or arrival time without verified rules and approval.

Marketing must rely on substantiated service coverage, supported vehicle categories, credentials and real availability. Do not claim universal repair capability, guaranteed arrival, manufacturer approval or safety outcomes without evidence.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate collision scenes, roadside danger, possible fuel leaks, smoke or fire, injury, stranded vulnerable people, disputes about keys or vehicle damage, insurance or warranty claims, payment disputes and requests for unsafe repair instructions.

## Included specialist add-ons

- Mobile mechanic
- Vehicle detailing
