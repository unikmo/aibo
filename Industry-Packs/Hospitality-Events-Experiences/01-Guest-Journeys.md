# Guest and customer journeys

## Shared journey
Awareness → consideration → booking/reservation → confirmation → pre-arrival/pre-event message → check-in/admission → on-site delivery and support → checkout/departure → follow-up → review/UGC (permission-gated) → repeat/referral/membership.

At each transition record: accountable person, system of record, guest expectation, consent/permission, cutoff, exception escalation, and measurable outcome. Soni may prepare drafts and internal coordination; **sending ordinary guest messages, making reservations, allocating scarce inventory, cancelling, refunding or changing staff schedules remain approval-gated** unless an explicit narrower owner delegation is later recorded.

## Four journey variants
| Variant | Key stages | Typical failure points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overnight stays | Search/channel → room type/rate check → reservation → pre-arrival requests → check-in → stay/housekeeping → checkout → review/rebook | Parity/availability conflicts, accessibility mismatch, maintenance block, early/late arrival |
| Day experiences & activities | Discovery → date/slot/participant selection → payment/waivers → pre-brief → check-in → activity → follow-up | Weather, minimum group, guide safety, no-show, unsuitable fitness/age |
| Ticketed/seated events | Event discovery → ticket/category/seat → confirmation → access/queue → programme → exit → next event | Fraud/duplicate tickets, peak ingress, fire capacity, accessibility, refunds |
| Private/corporate events & venues | Enquiry → requirements/site visit → proposal → negotiation/sign-off → deposits → production plan → event day → settlement/debrief | Competing holds, supplier contracts, AV/F&B dependencies, changes and safety |

## Acquisition and relationship tracks
- **Direct:** measure actual net economics, owned consent, ability to communicate, retention; no fabricated booking savings.
- **OTA / marketplace:** verify current channel terms, commissions, data-use and messaging rules; never move a reservation off-platform in violation of terms.
- **Corporate / group:** named organiser and guests; multi-stakeholder approvals, contracted room/seat blocks, deposit, attrition clauses and privacy separation.
- **Inbound/referral vs outbound:** record origin and intent; researched prospect outreach follows the central Soni delegation only when every recipient and jurisdictional check passes. Guest promotional email is **not** automatically covered by B2B outreach delegation.

## Operational handoffs
Never assume a confirmed booking without an authoritative confirmation ID. Flag stale availability, accidental duplicate reservation, overcapacity, unresolved accessibility request, guest distress, incident or health/safety issue for immediate human intervention.
