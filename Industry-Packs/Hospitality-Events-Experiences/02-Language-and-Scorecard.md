# Vocabulary, positioning and KPI scorecard

## Language
Tone: warm, concise, service-oriented and low-friction; avoid unverified superlatives or guaranteed experiences. Use: occupancy, available room nights, average daily rate (ADR), RevPAR, yield, booking window, cover, table turn, seat utilisation, no-show, walk-in, shoulder season, service recovery, review velocity, NPS, run-of-show and cancellation policy. Never treat hotel room, restaurant cover and event seat as interchangeable units.

## KPI definitions and evidence
| KPI | Definition / safeguards |
| --- | --- |
| Occupancy | Sold room nights ÷ sellable available room nights (specify out-of-order/excluded rooms and period) |
| Slot/seat utilisation | Consumed/booked capacity ÷ safely sellable capacity for same period (distinguish reserved vs attended) |
| ADR / ticket or cover value | Room revenue ÷ rooms sold; or relevant sales ÷ tickets/covers with refunds/tax handling stated |
| RevPAR / per-available-unit revenue | Room revenue ÷ available room nights; analogues must explicitly specify seat/slot denominators, not call every measure RevPAR |
| Direct booking mix | Direct confirmed bookings or revenue ÷ all confirmed bookings or revenue; disclose denominator, fees and channel attribution |
| Booking lead time | Arrival/event start date minus booking creation timestamp; show distribution, not only mean |
| Cancellation and no-show | Cancelled/absent reservations ÷ comparable confirmed reservations; define eligible cutoffs and units |
| Reviews | Volume, rating by platform, share of verified guests, response time, period and sample limitations |
| Repeat rate | Returning guests/members ÷ eligible identified guests in period, subject to privacy/identity limits |
| Satisfaction/recovery | Survey response rate, CSAT/NPS definitions; recovered cases ÷ valid recovery cases with evidence |
| Labour cost/revenue | Fully specified labour costs ÷ relevant net revenue; do not compare unlike cost bases |
| Yield / margin | Net contribution per available unit after channel fees, variable service costs, refunds and taxes where appropriate |

Report: source/system, as-of timestamp, market/location, reporting period, denominator, confidence, changes vs baseline and data gaps. Never invent actual occupancy or reviews. Seasonal comparisons use like-for-like periods and distinguish capacity changes.
