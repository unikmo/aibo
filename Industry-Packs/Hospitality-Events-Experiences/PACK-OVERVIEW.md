# Hospitality, Events & Experiences Edition — Core Knowledge Pack

**Scope:** Hotels, accommodation, restaurants/F&B, event venues, tour/activity providers, event agencies, and recurring experiences. This industry pack adds guidance to AIBO's shared core; **Soni remains the only customer-facing coordinator**. No new agent or independent authority is created.

## Activation and operating objective
Activate the **core pack as required** for a verified Hospitality, Events & Experiences project, then only add the applicable modules. Prioritise demand quality, perishable capacity yield, direct booking economics, end-to-end guest experience, service recovery and repeat business. Do not trade service quality, safety, lawful permissions, or truthful availability for occupancy or revenue.

**Read order:** `AGENTS.md` → selected project facts → `System/CONNECTIONS.md` → core `01`–`05` → selected `Add-ons/*/PACK-ADDON.md` → applicable templates. Do not read or mingle unrelated projects' guest data.

## Preflight evidence (unknown until verified)
Business type and venue/property footprint; market, seasonality and local regulations; guest segments; booking channels; approved inventory source and refresh timestamp; capacity/room types/table turns/staffing/accessible capacity; cancellation, refund and overbooking policies; rates/fees/taxes; review permissions; health/safety obligations; PMS, POS, channel manager and payment access; owner approval thresholds; opt-in for guest messaging and media use.

## Core modules
- `01-Guest-Journeys.md`: direct vs marketplace vs group tracks and four operating variants.
- `02-Language-and-Scorecard.md`: definitions and denominator-aware metrics.
- `03-Playbooks-and-Templates.md`: operational workflows, control points, reusable artifacts.
- `04-Connections-and-Approvals.md`: integrations, read/write controls and nondelegable decisions.
- `05-Soni-Routing-and-Activation.md`: composition, specialist briefs, precedence and example missions.
- `Templates/`: practical, evidence-labelled working forms.
- `Add-ons/`: six optional specialisms.
