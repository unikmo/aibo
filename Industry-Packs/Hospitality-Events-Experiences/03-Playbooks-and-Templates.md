# Applied playbooks and templates

1. **Demand calendar + seasonal offer matrix:** map demand by date/daypart/segment, events, booking pace, price rules and expected contribution. Draft offers only; pricing changes need owner approval.
2. **Reservation and pre-arrival:** verify booking identifier, event date/time/time zone, cancellation terms, accessibility requests and communication permissions. Stage confirmation and practical information; ordinary guest sends remain approval-gated.
3. **Run-of-show / service blueprint:** use timed checkpoints, staff responsibilities, handovers, supplier dependencies, capacity and escalation contacts. No unapproved staffing or guest capacity change.
4. **During-stay/experience messaging:** accurate directions, venue details and service assistance; triage urgent safety/medical incidents to on-site humans/emergency services rather than automated reassurance.
5. **Review and reputation:** classify verified feedback, research cause, produce a factual response draft and internal service fix; public replies need approval. Do not purchase/incentivise positive reviews or expose guest data.
6. **Incident and recovery:** log facts minimally, preserve evidence with access restrictions, notify responsible staff, recommend policy-compliant remedies; refunds, discounts and compensation need approval.
7. **Post-stay/event debrief:** reconcile planned vs actual occupancy/attendance/quality, incidents, costs, channel performance; collect reusable content **only after rights/consent checks**.
8. **Packages/experiences:** define included services, scarce capacity, partner costs, taxes, booking rules, margins, and alternative dates; no live offer before rate and inventory approval.

## Standard quality gates
Source-of-truth freshness; legal capacity and accessibility; contract/booking rules; owner thresholds; privacy, consent and guest image rights; human emergency escalation; independent QA of consequential changes; reconciliation of actions against provider confirmation. See `Templates/` for reusable records.
