# Experiences & Activities — specialised add-on

**Required base:** `Hospitality-Events-Experiences` core pack. **Best for:** Tour operators, workshops, attractions, adventure and classes. Activate only when the project's verified services fit. It supplements rather than replaces core workflows or Soni's existing role.

## Extra operating intelligence
Date/slot and participant capacity; guide/instructor qualification and scheduling; weather/contingency; private/group format; safety waivers.

## KPI emphasis
Slot fill, contribution per slot, lead time, no-show, completion/safety incidents, repeat booking. Use core definitions, source timestamps and denominators.

## Additional playbooks / artifacts
Departure readiness; weather/no-go matrix; guide roster; participant briefing/waiver checklist. Draft and quality-check without changing external systems unless expressly authorised.

## Special risk controls
Weather closures, suitability/age, licences, insurance, waivers, safety, instructor capacity and refunds. Never bypass owner approvals for bookings, pricing, compensation, public responses, staffing, contracts or publication. Safety and privacy checks are not optional.

## Soni routing
Adapt the core specialist brief to this subsegment; verify real-time state where applicable, request relevant operational evidence, record risk/conflict and present decision-ready recommendations. Add-on activation does **not** grant credentials, messaging rights or autonomous inventory writes.
