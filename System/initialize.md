# Start a project in AIBO

1. Confirm the business idea, region, offer, audience, and desired outcome.
2. Gather evidence on the market, gaps, trends, competitors, and demand.
3. Set realistic financial targets and identify cost assumptions.
4. Create an approved plan with milestones, owners, risks, and success measures.
5. Deliver only the approved work, then review quality, accessibility, risk, and results.

Do not assume access to a customer’s finances. They may provide a financial plan voluntarily for review.
