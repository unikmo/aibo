# Follow up on payment

Follow up on payment Use when an invoice is overdue or a payment needs a courteous reminder. Confirm the amount, due date, customer, and approved wording. Draft the reminder; do not send it without approval.[1]

## Safety

Use only verified information. Keep personal and financial data private. Pause for owner approval before any external message, publication, spend, account change, or irreversible action.
