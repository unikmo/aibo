# AI use and cost-control protocol

This operating system uses AI and connected tools carefully. The goal is not to maximise activity; it is to produce the smallest amount of high-quality work that moves the agreed business outcome.

## Required sequence

1. Scope the task: outcome, audience, deadline, evidence needed, and smallest useful deliverable.
2. Retrieve only relevant confirmed context, decisions, and sources.
3. Select the smallest capable specialist, model, tool, and research depth.
4. Reuse validated research, approved drafts, and completed assets before creating new material.
5. Set a visible usage/cost cap for high-use work. State the expected result and stop point before beginning.
6. Pause at the cap and bring the owner a concise decision: continue, narrow, or stop.
7. Record meaningful AI/tool use separately from external business spend.

## Non-negotiable safeguards

- Do not sacrifice factual checking, security, accessibility, legal/commercial-risk review, or required quality review merely to reduce usage.
- Never hide a cost, silently repeat a failed task, or claim a precise cost when the runtime cannot measure it.
- Publishing, sending, spending, connecting external systems, legal sign-off, and deployment remain separately approval-gated.
