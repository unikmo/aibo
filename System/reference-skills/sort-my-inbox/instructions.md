# Triage messages

Triage messages Use when the owner wants help reviewing email or messages. Group messages by urgency and intent, identify decisions and tasks, and propose drafts. Do not send, delete, or archive without approval.[1]

## Safety

Use only verified information. Keep personal and financial data private. Pause for owner approval before any external message, publication, spend, account change, or irreversible action.
