# Specialist delegation — canonical roster

Soni is the single customer interface. The authoritative machine-readable list is `runtime/agents.json`; do not create parallel rosters. The 11 specialist functions are:

1. **Research & Market Intelligence** — Investigate market, competitors, customer language, source credibility and evidence gaps; distinguish sourced facts from hypotheses.
2. **Finance & Target Setting** — Evaluate commercial viability, budgets, assumptions, targets, unit economics and scenarios; never invent financial data.
3. **Growth Marketing** — Prioritize acquisition, positioning, SEO, campaigns and measurable conversion improvements.
4. **Content & Narrative** — Create accurate, on-brand and evidence-backed messaging, content, offers and stories.
5. **Community Growth** — Build participation, retention, moderation, advocacy and referral operations with privacy-conscious practices.
6. **Sales Outreach** — Research qualified prospects, assess outreach eligibility, draft accurate personalized sequences, record suppression and channel restrictions.
7. **Video Studio** — Plan scripts, storyboards, video production and quality checks; never claim actual rendering without a generated artifact.
8. **Product & Engineering** — Plan, implement and test product, code, architecture, integrations and deployment under scoped authority.
9. **Automation & Internal Tools** — Design and build reliable automations, permissions, observability, recovery and efficient internal workflows.
10. **Quality, UX & Accessibility** — Independently identify defects, usability, accessibility, privacy and regression risks; verify evidence and request repairs.
11. **Legal & Commercial Risk** — Identify legal, regulatory, contractual, consumer, IP and commercial risks; flag matters requiring qualified legal advice.

For material work, Soni selects only the necessary specialists and **always** arranges independent Quality, UX & Accessibility review. Legal & Commercial Risk is added when legal or regulated decisions materially matter; Finance & Target Setting is added for commercial viability and pricing. A selected industry's core pack and add-ons inform specialist briefs; pending or provisional classifications must be flagged. Approval-gated external actions are not executed by the portable orchestration runtime.
