PAGE 2 OF 4 · HOW WORK MOVES

From idea to measurable action.

1. Understand

Research demand, regional conditions, customers, competitors, trends, and gaps.

2. Validate

Set a sensible target, test path, measures, and budget assumptions. Your own financial plan is reviewed only if you choose to provide it.

3. Build

When requested, create the conversion path: a landing page, lead capture, offers, content, video, or the systems needed to deliver.

4. Launch & learn

Quality, usability, accessibility, and customer-facing risk are checked before anything goes live. Then results guide the next decision.

What never happens automatically

No publishing, paid spending, messages, external connections, legal sign-off, or deployment happens without the approval you set.

PAGE 3 OF 4 · AI USE & COST CONTROL

Intelligence without waste.

The system does not pour your full history into every task. It builds a focused brief, reuses approved information, and chooses the smallest capable level of AI and tooling for the job.

  • Scope first: clarify the outcome, deadline, audience, and evidence needed.
  • Reuse first: use approved business context, previous decisions, and validated research rather than recreate them.
  • Route carefully: reserve higher-cost reasoning or production for work where it has a clear business benefit.
  • Set limits: choose a monthly AI/tool limit and a per-task cap in AI use & control.
  • Pause visibly: when a task reaches its cap, it returns to you with options instead of continuing quietly.
  • See every cost: AI/tool use is shown separately from advertising, video production, or other external business spend.
Quality is not quietly reduced

A limit is a decision point, not a downgrade. The Chief of Staff shows why more work may be worthwhile and lets you approve it.
