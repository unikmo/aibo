Quick answers

What does “One Soni” mean?

Soni is the single business partner coordinating the specialist agents. In AIBO Custom, one Soni can support many separate project workspaces.

Will a new project replace an old one?

No. New projects are additive only. Creating or initializing a project does not replace, merge, or delete existing project folders or files.

What does Initialize do?

Initialize saves the project-specific details Soni needs to begin: the name, location or region, and the project’s main goal.

Does Initialize send anything?

No. Initialization in this local preview only saves setup details in the workspace. It does not send messages, publish content, spend money, or trigger live AI execution.

Where do industry, core pack, and add-ons live?

In AIBO Standard they belong to the license. In AIBO Custom they belong to each individual project workspace.

How do I resume setup?

Select Resume setup on the project card. Soni keeps the last saved step and your entries on this computer. Finish setup after reviewing the summary.

What happens when I select Ask Soni?

Soni routes the task to matching specialists and a quality review, then shows the response and work history. The Demo provider does not contact AI or external services. Other providers require explicit consent and may process business context through that provider.

Why are some packs marked pending?

That means the project still needs a verified knowledge-pack decision. AIBO should not invent or auto-activate a paid pack.

Important

The current Control Centre is the foundation of the product. It is not yet proof of live agent execution, mailbox actions, or production publishing.
